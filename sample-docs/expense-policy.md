# Expense Policy

Kestrel Lane Software is a fictional company. This policy is sample data for a demo project.

This policy explains what the company pays for when you travel or spend money on company business, and how to claim it back.

## Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll run.

### Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense above 25 EUR. A card statement on its own is not accepted as a receipt. For lost receipts, fill in the missing receipt form; this is allowed at most twice per year.

### Approvals

Your line manager approves claims up to 1,000 EUR. Claims above 1,000 EUR also need approval from the finance team.

## Travel

Book all flights and trains through the company travel portal so that the booking appears in the duty-of-care tracker.

### Flights

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours. Business class is not reimbursed.

### Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 150 EUR in most cities and 220 EUR in high-cost cities listed in the travel portal. The limit includes taxes but excludes breakfast.

### Ground transport

Use public transport where practical. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed for trips to and from airports, or when travelling between 22:00 and 06:00. Using your own car is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre.

## Meals

When travelling overnight, meals are reimbursed up to a daily allowance of 60 EUR. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Team meals with a client require a list of attendees on the claim.

## Home office

Every employee who works from home at least two days a week receives a one-time home office budget of 400 EUR for a desk, a chair or similar equipment. Items bought with this budget remain your property.

## Not reimbursable

Fines, traffic or parking penalties, personal entertainment subscriptions, upgrades paid for with personal loyalty points, and lost personal items are never reimbursed.
