# Customer Support and Service Levels

This document describes support for Tidepool, a fictional product of the fictional company Kestrel Lane Software. It is sample data for a demo project.

## Support channels

Free plan customers get help through the community forum and the help centre. Team plan customers can also open tickets by email. Business plan customers additionally get live chat and a named customer success contact.

## Support hours

Email and chat support are staffed from 08:00 to 20:00 Central European Time, Monday to Friday. Priority 1 incidents for Business customers are handled around the clock, every day of the year.

## Priority levels

### Priority 1

The service is down or unusable for most users, with no workaround. First response within 1 hour for Business customers and 4 hours for Team customers.

### Priority 2

A major feature is not working, but a workaround exists. First response within 4 business hours for Business customers and 1 business day for Team customers.

### Priority 3

Questions, minor bugs and feature requests. First response within 2 business days for all paid plans.

## Uptime commitment

Tidepool targets 99.9 percent monthly uptime for the web app and API. Planned maintenance is announced at least 5 days in advance on the status page and is not counted as downtime.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime for a Business workspace falls below 99.9 percent, the customer can claim a service credit of 10 percent of that month's fee. Below 99.0 percent, the credit is 25 percent. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month and are applied to the next invoice; they are not paid out in cash.

## Escalation

If a ticket is not progressing, customers can ask for escalation in the ticket. Escalated tickets are reviewed by a support team lead within 1 business day.
