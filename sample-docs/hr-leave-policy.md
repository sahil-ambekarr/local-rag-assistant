# Leave Policy

Kestrel Lane Software is a fictional company. This document was written as sample data for a demo project.

Policy owner: People Operations. Applies to all permanent employees. Contractors follow the terms in their contract.

## Annual leave

Every permanent employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. New joiners receive a pro-rated amount for their first year, rounded up to the nearest half day.

Leave requests go through the HR portal. Requests of five working days or more must be submitted at least three weeks in advance so the team can plan cover. Shorter requests need one week of notice.

### Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Any balance above 5 days is lost at year end unless your manager and People Operations approved an exception in writing before 1 December.

### Leave encashment

Unused annual leave is not paid out during employment. When you leave the company, the remaining balance for the current year is paid out at your base daily rate.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over.

Tell your manager before your usual start time on the first day of absence. Record the absence in the HR portal within two working days of returning.

### Medical certificate

A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 3 consecutive working days. Upload it to the HR portal; do not email it to your manager.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Both can be taken any time within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

Give People Operations at least 8 weeks of notice before the planned start date. Parental leave can be split into at most two blocks.

## Bereavement leave

Up to 5 paid days are granted after the death of an immediate family member and up to 2 paid days for other relatives. No documentation is required.

## Unpaid leave and sabbaticals

After 3 years of continuous service, employees may request an unpaid sabbatical of between 1 and 3 months. Sabbaticals need director approval and at least 3 months of notice. Health insurance continues during an approved sabbatical.
