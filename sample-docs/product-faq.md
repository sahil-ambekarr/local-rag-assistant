# Tidepool Product FAQ

Tidepool is a fictional task board product made by the fictional company Kestrel Lane Software. This FAQ is sample data for a demo project.

## Plans and pricing

### What plans are available?

Tidepool has three plans. Free supports up to 5 users and 3 boards. Team costs 6 EUR per user per month and has unlimited boards. Business costs 12 EUR per user per month and adds single sign-on, audit logs and priority support.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day trial of the Business plan. No payment card is needed to start the trial. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan.

### Do you offer discounts?

Annual billing is 20 percent cheaper than monthly billing. Registered non-profit organisations get a further 50 percent discount on the Team plan.

## Features

### Can I import tasks from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Boards can be imported from a CSV file with up to 5,000 rows. Each row becomes one card, and columns can be mapped to title, description, assignee, due date and labels.

### Does Tidepool work offline?

The desktop app keeps the last 7 days of board activity available offline. Changes made offline are synced when the connection returns. The web app needs a connection.

### What is the maximum attachment size?

Attachments can be up to 100 MB per file on paid plans and 10 MB per file on the Free plan.

### Which integrations are available?

Tidepool integrates with calendar apps, chat tools and code hosting platforms. A public REST API and outgoing webhooks are available on the Team and Business plans.

## Data and security

### Where is my data stored?

Workspace data is stored in data centres in the European Union. Backups are taken every 6 hours and kept for 30 days.

### Can I export my data?

Workspace admins can export all boards, cards and comments as JSON at any time from the admin settings page. The export link stays valid for 24 hours.

### What happens when I delete my workspace?

A deleted workspace can be restored by an admin for 30 days. After 30 days, all data is permanently removed, including backups.

## Account management

### How do I cancel?

Admins can cancel a paid plan from the billing page. The plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and no partial refunds are given for monthly plans.
