OFFICE GUIDE - RIVERSIDE OFFICE

Kestrel Lane Software is a fictional company. This guide is sample data for a demo project.

Opening hours. The Riverside office is open from 07:00 to 21:00 on working days. The building is closed on weekends and public holidays. Your badge works only during opening hours; for weekend access, ask Facilities at least 2 working days in advance.

Badges. You receive your access badge from reception on your first day. A lost badge must be reported to Facilities the same day so it can be deactivated. A replacement badge costs 15 EUR, which is deducted from your next payroll, unless the badge was stolen and you can show a police report.

Visitors. Register visitors in the visitor system by 17:00 on the working day before their visit. Visitors must sign the confidentiality form at reception and must be accompanied by their host at all times. Visitors receive a temporary paper badge that is valid for one day.

Desks and meeting rooms. Desks are not assigned. Book a desk in the desk booking app up to 14 days in advance. Meeting rooms can be booked for up to 4 hours at a time. If you do not check in to a booked room within 15 minutes of the start time, the booking is released automatically.

Parking. The office has 40 parking spaces, allocated through a weekly lottery that closes on Thursday at 12:00. Four spaces with electric car chargers are reserved for electric vehicles and can be used for at most 4 hours per day. Bicycle parking is free and does not need a booking; showers are on the ground floor.

Kitchen. Coffee, tea and fruit are free. The fridge is cleared every Friday at 16:00; anything left inside is thrown away. Please label your food with your name and the date.

Lost property. Items found in the office are kept at reception for 30 days. After 30 days, unclaimed items are donated.

Emergencies. The fire assembly point is the car park across the road at the north entrance. In a medical emergency, call the emergency number first and then reception. First aiders are listed on the noticeboard next to each lift. A fire drill takes place twice a year without advance notice.
