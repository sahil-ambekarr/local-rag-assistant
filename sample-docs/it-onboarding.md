# IT Onboarding Guide

Kestrel Lane Software is a fictional company. This guide is sample data for a demo project.

Welcome. This guide covers your first week with company equipment and accounts. The IT service desk is open 08:00 to 18:00 on working days and can be reached through the #it-help chat channel or the service desk portal.

## Your laptop

New engineers receive a 14-inch laptop with 32 GB of RAM. Other roles receive a 14-inch laptop with 16 GB of RAM. Laptops are shipped to your home address five working days before your start date.

### First login

Connect to a wired or home Wi-Fi network, sign in with the temporary password from your welcome email, and change it immediately. Full-disk encryption is turned on automatically during first login and takes about 40 minutes; keep the laptop plugged in.

### Software installation

Approved software is installed from the Self Service app. If you need a tool that is not listed there, raise a software request in the service desk portal. Requests are reviewed within 2 working days.

## Accounts and passwords

Your company account gives access to email, chat, the HR portal and the code hosting platform.

### Password rules

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords do not expire on a schedule, but you must change yours immediately if you suspect it was exposed. Use the company password manager for every work credential.

### Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory. Enrol the authenticator app on your phone on day one. If you lose your phone, call the service desk to have your MFA reset; a reset requires a video call to verify your identity.

## VPN

You need the VPN only to reach the internal build servers and the staging environment. Email, chat and the HR portal work without it. The VPN client is pre-installed and connects with your company account.

## Equipment

You may order one external monitor, one keyboard, one mouse and one headset in your first month through the equipment catalogue in the service desk portal. Items arrive within 10 working days.

### Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the service desk within 1 hour of noticing. The device will be locked and wiped remotely.

## Returning equipment

When you leave the company, return your laptop and accessories within 7 days of your last working day using the prepaid courier box that IT sends you.
