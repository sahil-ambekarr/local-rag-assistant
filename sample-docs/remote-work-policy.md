# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Kestrel Lane Software is a fictional company. This policy is sample data for a demo project.

## Who it applies to

This policy applies to all employees after they complete their probation period of 3 months. During probation, employees work from the office at least 3 days a week so they can get to know their team.

## Hybrid schedule

The standard arrangement is hybrid: employees are expected in the office on 2 anchor days per week, which each team agrees on. The remaining days can be worked from home.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local office time. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours. Outside core hours you can arrange your working time freely as long as you work your contracted hours.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Tell your manager and People Operations at least 2 weeks before you travel, so that tax and insurance rules can be checked. Some countries are excluded for legal reasons; the list is kept on the intranet.

## Fully remote roles

A small number of roles are advertised as fully remote. Employees in these roles are invited to a company meetup twice a year, and the company pays travel and accommodation for it.

## Workspace requirements

Your home workspace must have a reliable internet connection of at least 20 Mbit/s and a place where you can take confidential calls without being overheard. Do not work on company data from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.

## Internet allowance

Employees on the hybrid schedule receive a monthly internet allowance of 25 EUR paid through payroll. Fully remote employees receive 40 EUR per month.

## Ending a remote arrangement

The company may ask an employee to return to the hybrid schedule with 4 weeks of notice if the arrangement is not working, for example because of repeated missed meetings.
